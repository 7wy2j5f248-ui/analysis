# Researcher-Designated AI Stage 1 Analytical Contract

## Purpose and Governing Relationship

This contract defines the direct analytical relationship between the researcher and the AI explicitly selected for this analytical run.

The designated AI receives the complete frozen analytical contract and the authorized frozen source. Its exact provider response reaches the researcher without any external AI or application-level analytical intermediary.

RESEARCHER → FROZEN CONTRACT AND SOURCE → DESIGNATED AI → EXACT RESPONSE → RESEARCHER

---

# Universal Direct Analytical Relationship

## U1 Exclusive Analytical Relationship

For this analytical run, the only analytical parties are the researcher and the exact provider and model selected by the researcher. The provider-delivered AI system is accepted as the designated AI in its complete API form.

## U2 One Designated AI Recipient

PLI sends the analytical contract and authorized source only to the designated AI for this run.

No second AI receives the request or response for validation, verification, review, scoring, criticism, repair, rewriting, parsing, comparison, approval, rejection, retry selection, or replacement.

## U3 No Analytical Intermediary

No application rule, deterministic analytical quality test, secondary model, validator, reviewer, monitor, repairer, gatekeeper, projection agent, or substitute model may judge or alter the designated AI's analytical work before or after the researcher receives it.

## U4 Complete Contract Delivery

Every applicable analytical term is assembled into one researcher-visible request and supplied directly to the designated AI.

No hidden analytical instruction, codebook, exclusion rule, prompt, or qualitative standard may be added by PLI.

## U5 Exact Output and Researcher Authority

PLI preserves the designated AI's exact provider response, including weak, unexpected, malformed, incomplete, empty, or failed output and the provider's technical diagnostics.

Only the researcher decides analytical adequacy, whether another run is warranted, or whether a different model will be selected.

## U6 Every Analytical Stage

These universal provisions apply to Stage 1, Stage 2A, later Code Category and Theme harmonization, Stage 3 interpretation, reanalysis, and every future analytical stage.

A later stage may perform only its separately defined task and may not reopen or repair an earlier stage.

## U7 Content Neutral Infrastructure

PLI may authenticate, transport, freeze, hash, store, retrieve, display, protect privacy, record provider technical status, prevent duplicate writes, enforce cohort barriers, and execute researcher-authorized workflow transitions.

These operations may not evaluate analytical quality or create analytical content.

## U8 Undefined Stages Cannot Run

No analytical stage may execute until the researcher has approved its complete model-facing task, source boundary, output contract, provider, exact model, settings, and transition conditions.

# R1 Authoritative Case Evidence Contract

## R1.1 Formally Completed Case

A case may enter Stage 1 only after the researcher-defined interview completion condition has been satisfied and the case has been closed for analytical use.

Inactivity alone does not create analytical completion unless the researcher expressly defines it as such.

## R1.2 Anonymous Case Identity and Lineage

Every analytical source is bound to an anonymous case identifier, research project, applicable research design version, and analytical run.

Real-world interviewee identity is not supplied to the analytical model.

## R1.3 Frozen Analytical Transcript

The analytical source for a case is the exact transcript snapshot frozen for that run.

The source cannot be silently reloaded from later edited records or reconstructed from current project settings after the request is made.

## R1.4 Interviewee Evidence Boundary

Stage 1 analytical evidence consists of interviewee-authored substantive responses in the frozen analytical transcript.

Interviewer turns may provide only researcher-authorized contextual framing and may never become interviewee Meaning Units or interviewee claims.

## R1.5 Multilingual Source Authority

For a non-English interview, the frozen English translation is the analytical text used to generate Meaning Units, Codes, Categories, and Themes.

The exact original-language transcript remains preserved and linked for verification.

Missing translation is disclosed and is not silently replaced by an inferred translation.

## R1.6 Evidence Traceability

Every analytical evidence record remains traceable to its exact frozen source turn or turns and exact source passage.

PLI and later stages may not invent or substitute evidence.

## R1.7 Demographic Separation

Interviewee characteristics may be stored and displayed in the factual case record but are not supplied to Stage 1 or Stage 2 analytical generation.

They become analytically active only under an expressly approved later-stage contract.

## R1.8 No Prior Analytical Input

Stage 1 receives no previous analysis, legacy report, corpus vocabulary, predetermined codebook, output from another model, or materialized software reconstruction unless the researcher explicitly creates a different analytical method and approves that source in a separate run.

## R1.9 Independent Source for Every Run

Every separately authorized analytical run records its own exact frozen source.

A new run never edits, overwrites, or retroactively changes the source or output of an earlier run.

# R2 Stage 1 Analysis Request Contract

# R2.2 Global Analysis Rules

## R2.2.1 Case Independence

Analyze each completed case independently.

Do not compare the case with another interviewee and do not use a cross-case vocabulary during Stage 1.

## R2.2.2 Authoritative Source

Use only the frozen analytical transcript and the approved project context supplied in this request.

## R2.2.3 Evidence Traceability

Keep every Meaning Unit and every higher analytical claim linked through the explicit evidence chain to the exact source turn or turns.

## R2.2.4 Evidence Grounding

Do not introduce unsupported facts, motives, explanations, causal claims, theoretical claims, or interviewee characteristics absent from the authorized source.

## R2.2.5 Bottom-Up Progression

Develop the connected analysis in this order:

Transcript → Meaning Units → Preliminary Codes → Preliminary Categories → Preliminary Tentative Themes

## R2.2.6 No Forced Reduction

There is no target minimum or maximum number of Meaning Units, Codes, Categories, or Themes.

Preserve the complexity supported by the case.

## R2.2.7 No Cross-Case Standardization

Use terminology appropriate to this case.

Do not force agreement with another case or a corpus-wide codebook during Stage 1.

## R2.2.8 English Analytical Output

Produce the analytical objects and case summary in English while retaining exact source provenance to the frozen transcript.

## R2.2.9 No Gatekeeper

Return the analysis directly.

No second AI or application analytical quality mechanism will validate, repair, score, approve, reject, or replace it.

## R2.2.10 Exact Response Preservation

The exact provider response is the authoritative Stage 1 output for this run.

Presentation may not substitute a reconstructed analytical version.
